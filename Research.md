# Research Work — Miura Fold Based Origami Solar Panel

This document summarizes the research and engineering insights used during the design and development of a Miura fold-based deployable solar panel system. The aim was to create a compact, efficient, and lightweight solar panel structure using the principles of origami, specifically Miura-ori folds, suitable for aerospace and portable terrestrial applications.

---

## 1. Objective

To design and simulate a foldable solar panel system based on the Miura-ori origami principle, allowing for efficient deployment and compact storage, particularly suited for applications in space missions or compact renewable energy systems on Earth.

---

## 2. Understanding Miura Fold

The Miura fold is a method of folding a flat surface into a compact form using a tessellated pattern of parallelograms. Invented by Japanese astrophysicist Koryo Miura, the pattern enables expansion and contraction through a single degree of motion.

### Key Characteristics:
- Single-degree-of-freedom motion
- Uniform deployment with minimal effort
- Structural rigidity in expanded form
- Compactness in folded form
- Efficient packaging of large surfaces

---

## 3. Applications in Aerospace

Miura fold has been adopted in aerospace engineering due to its lightweight and efficient deployability. NASA has used this technique in:

- Spacecraft solar arrays
- Foldable antennas
- Membrane structures
- Solar sails

The method reduces the payload volume and ensures quick deployment in space environments where mechanical simplicity and reliability are essential.

---

## 4. Design Methodology

The design was created in Autodesk Fusion 360, focusing on:

- Accurate crease patterns
- Control over mountain and valley fold lines
- Defined angles between segments
- Simulated folding motion using assembly joints
- Exported in multiple formats (.f3d, .step, .stl) for analysis and testing

The design follows the geometric principles of Miura-ori, using parallelogram-based tiles connected in a way that folding occurs in a synchronized motion.

---

## 5. Materials and Simulation

Theoretical studies were conducted on suitable materials for real-world prototypes, considering:

- Flexibility and elasticity
- Weight-to-strength ratio
- Fatigue resistance for repeated folding

Simulation tools in Fusion 360 were optionally used to study joint movement and simulate folding action.
